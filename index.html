WHY CHOOSE SHIVAM WIRES?

At SHIVAM WIRES, we power homes, offices, retail complexes, and factories with high-conductivity copper cables built for maximum safety and durability.

100% Pure Copper

Manufactured using high-purity annealed electrolytic copper conductors to ensure low resistance, zero overheating, and energy savings.

Fire Retardant (FR / FRLS)

Engineered with flame-retardant and low-smoke PVC/XLPE insulation that prevents fire propagation during electrical short circuits.

Heavy Duty & Long Lasting

Tough, moisture-resistant, and abrasion-proof outer sheathing designed for underground, industrial, and concealed wiring longevity.
